“Not Your Footballs!” Newly Formed Union of Non-Permanent Maruti-Suzuki Employees Intensifies Agitation, Calls Out Abuse of Contract Workers

Thousands of current and former non-permanent Maruti-Suzuki workers gathered at the Gurgaon DC office on 10th January 2025 to submit their charter of demands — challenging the company's hire-and-fire labour practices and demanding equal pay and permanent employment alongside the already-protesting terminated workers.

By Mouli Sharma
11 January 2025 · 6 min read

Following is the official press release of the Maruti Suzuki Asthayi Workers Sangh for their demonstration outside the DC office in Rajiv Chowk, Gurgaon on the 10th of January 2025 , where thousands of workers from several states joined those in Haryana, which has been the epicentre of the Maruti Movement since 2011. The agitation of Non-Permanent workers started on 5th January, and stands hand in hand with the already protesting terminated workers union, the Maruti Workers Sturggle Committee, which has been on an indefinite strike near the company's Manesar plant since September 18th 2024.

January 10, Gurgaon: Thousands of current and erstwhile non-permanent workers of Maruti Suzuki gathered at the Gurgaon DC office to submit their collective charter of demands to the Labour Department challenging the illegal labour practices of the company. The workers have organised themselves under the Maruti Suzuki Asthayi Mazdoor Sangh, launched on the 5th of January, 2025 in a mass meeting held at Krishna Chowk, Gurgaon. The charter of demands was also submitted to the company management on January 9, 2025.

The labour department has scheduled a tripartite meeting with the company management and the Sangh on January 31, 2025. The demonstration also submitted a memorandum to the Chief Minister of Haryana and declared a massive mobilization and workers march in IMT Manesar on the 30th of January. A working committee of non-permanent workers from different states and representatives from among the Maruti terminated workers is leading the agitation. The workers have demanded an end to the reliance on various categories of temporary workers for regular production in all plants of the automobile giant. They are demanding permanent employment for work of a permanent nature, equal pay for equal work, hiring of temporary workers previously and presently employed by the company to all the plants as permanent workers including the new plant in Kharkhoda, Sonipat and ensuring useful and recognized training of Maruti Student trainees and apprentices. Non-permanent workers arriving into the area for the agitation have been camping at the protest site of terminated Maruti Workers at the Manesar Tehsil near IMT Manesar Chowk, seven kilometers from the company gate. “Workers are not footballs to be kicked out at your whim!” Sumit, a temporary worker from Bahadurgarh gave the movement its defining metaphor of temporary workers being footballs kicked around from company to company. The image comes from his own 12 year long experience in the automobile industry where he has worked in the Hero car plant in Noida and the Hero bike plant in Manesar besides various stints in Maruti Suzuki.
