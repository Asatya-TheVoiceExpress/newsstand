Dictate

A photo essay on autocratic politics and the suppression of thought — the first resource any authoritarian strips from a people is not dignity but the capacity for independent thinking. Photographs and text by Kanika Solanki, journalism student at the University of Delhi.

By Kanika Solanki
10 September 2023 · 1 min read

The identifying trait of autocratic politics, the most elementary means of taking control of a people, is cutting out their resources. And the first of these to go, perhaps even before dignity & survival, is thought. And the capacity to hold on to it.
